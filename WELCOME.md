# Welcome to the Grove 🌱

Hey there, traveler! 👋  
I’m glad you’ve stepped into the Grove—a place where ideas grow like seeds in fertile soil, where imagination is nurtured, and collaboration feels like an adventure.

Think of the Grove as your own living world. Here, you can plant ideas, tend to them with friends, watch them sprout, and eventually harvest the fruits of your creativity. Along the way, you’ll earn experience, unlock achievements, and take part in exciting events that make the journey feel like a game.

Let’s get you oriented so you can start planting your first seed right away.

---

## 🌍 Discover the Worlds

The Grove is divided into **Worlds**—thematic realms that give your ideas a unique flavor. Each world has its own vibe, icon, and description:

- **Forest** 🌲 – For natural, earthy concepts.
- **Digital** 💻 – For tech, code, and digital art.
- **Obsidian** ⚫ – For bold, edgy, and experimental ideas.
- …and more to explore!

When you enter a world, you’ll see the ideas (we call them **Creations**) that others have planted there. You can **Quick Plant** a new idea straight from the world’s page—just give it a title and watch it appear.

---

## 💡 Plant Your First Idea (Creation)

An **Idea** (or Creation) is the heart of the Grove. It lives in a world and grows through your contributions.

When you create an idea, you choose:
- **Title** – The name of your seed.
- **World** – Where it will grow.
- **Mode** – Personal (solo) or Team (invite collaborators).
- **Public/Private** – Share with everyone or keep it just for you and invited friends.

Inside each idea, you’ll find:
- **Timeline** – A chronological log of your entries: text notes, images, links, and voice recordings.
- **Project Chat** – A dedicated space to discuss the idea with your team (if in team mode).
- **Task Board** – To‑dos, priorities, and assignees to keep progress on track.
- **Status** – Watch your idea evolve from Seed → Growing → Thriving → Launching → Shipped.

Tip: Try adding a voice note or an image to see how multimedia entries enrich your timeline.

---

## 🤝 Collaborate & Grow Together

Ideas are better when tended with others.

### Invite Collaborators
Switch your idea to **Team Mode**, then head to the **Members** tab. Here you can:
- Invite friends by searching their name or email.
- Assign roles: **Owner** (you), **Admin** (helps manage), or **Member** (contributor).
- Set optional deadlines and XP rewards for each role to motivate progress.

### Join Someone Else’s Idea
Spot an idea you love? You can send a **Collaboration Request** from the idea’s page. The owner will review and, if they accept, you’ll join as a member with the role they choose.

### Communicate
- Use the **Project Chat** inside each idea for team discussions.
- For private conversations, go to the **Messages** section and start a **Direct Message** with any community member.

---

## 🏆 Earn XP, Level Up, and Collect Achievements

The Grove loves to reward your activity. Every action you take grants **Experience Points (XP)**:

- Creating an idea: +50 XP
- Adding an entry (text, image, link, audio): +10‑20 XP
- Liking a creation: +5 XP
- Commenting: +15 XP
- Accepting a collaboration request: +75 XP
- Completing a task: +10‑30 XP
- Participating in events: variable XP

As your XP grows, so does your **Level** (every 100 XP = a new level). Your level is displayed on your profile and in chats—a badge of your dedication.

### Achievements (Badges)
Special milestones unlock **Achievements**, each with its own icon and XP bonus. Examples:
- **First Creation** 🎉 – Plant your very first idea.
- **Social Butterfly** 🦋 – Send 10 direct messages.
- **Explorer** 🌍 – Create ideas in three different worlds.
- **Mentor** 🧑‍🏫 – Help five newcomers get their collaboration requests accepted.
- **Legend** 🏆 – Reach level 10.

Check your profile to see which achievements you’ve earned and which are waiting for you.

---

## 🎖️ Join Events (Hosted by Admins)

From time to time, the Grove’s administrators (that could be you!) host **Events**—time‑bound collaborations with themes, goals, and extra XP rewards.

Events appear in the **Events** section. You can:
- View upcoming and past events.
- Join an event and optionally link a creation you worked on for it.
- Earn the event’s XP reward upon completion (marked by you or the admin).

If you’re an admin, you can create your own events via the **Events** API (or future UI) to rally the community around a challenge or celebration.

---

## 🖼️ Personalize Your Profile

Your profile is your Grove identity. Make it yours by:
- Uploading an **avatar picture** (your face, a logo, or any image that represents you).
- Checking your **XP, level, and achievements**.
- Seeing your email, role, and user ID.

To change your avatar, go to your profile page, click the avatar area, select an image, and save. Your new picture will appear across the Grove—next to your name in chats, comments, and more.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Sign in** (via GitHub, Google, or email) – you’ll be redirected to the home feed.
2. **Explore Worlds** – click the Worlds link in the nav to see all realms.
3. **Plant an Idea** – choose a world, give it a title, and decide if you want to go solo or team up.
4. **Add Entries** – use the composer at the bottom of the idea page to add text, images, links, or voice notes.
5. **Invite Friends** – switch to Team Mode and start collaborating.
6. **Chat & Task** – use the project chat and task board to stay aligned.
7. **Watch Your XP Grow** – every action adds up; check your profile often.
8. **Join Events** – look for active events in the Events section for bonus XP and community fun.

Welcome to the Grove, traveler. May your ideas flourish. 🌿
